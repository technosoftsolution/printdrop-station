Terms, Privacy & Refund Policies

Compliant with Indian digital commerce and payment gateway guidelines.

1. Refund & Cancellation Policy

If printed copies are smudged, jammed, or defective due to printer malfunction or hardware error, customers are entitled to an immediate free reprint or a full 100% refund. Refund requests are processed within 24 to 48 hours directly to the original payment source (UPI/Card). Once print jobs are successfully produced per customer specifications, cancellations cannot be made for customized printed materials.

2. Privacy & Data Security Policy

PrintDrop adheres to a strict Zero-Resident Security Protocol. Files uploaded by customers are used solely for the immediate print job execution and are permanently shredded and purged from local disk storage immediately after printing. No customer documents are retained, shared, or archived.

3. Terms & Conditions

Customers must own or hold proper authorization to reproduce the materials submitted for printing. PrintDrop reserves the right to decline printing of unauthorized government IDs, counterfeits, or unlawful documents. All payments processed via online gateways (Razorpay) are subject to standard banking settlement terms.
